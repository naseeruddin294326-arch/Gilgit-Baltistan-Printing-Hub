Draft policy outline only. The business owner must review and finalize these terms before accepting real orders or payments.

Custom printing prices depend on specifications. A final quote and artwork approval process must be agreed before production starts.

Delivery area, estimated timing, handoff point and any delivery charges must be confirmed with the customer. Do not promise a delivery date until verified.
