What can I order?

Planned products include wedding cards, birthday and event stationery, banners and flex, visiting cards, and custom designs.

How are prices calculated?

Price depends on quantity, dimensions, material, finishing and design work. The team will confirm a quote before production.

Do you deliver across Gilgit-Baltistan?

Delivery is planned for supported areas. Availability, timing and any applicable costs must be confirmed for each order.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes, the design studio lets you enter a title, category, colour and message as a frontend preview. It is not yet a professional print-file generator.

Can I pay online now?

No. Payment options shown on this prototype are not active. Do not send money through this demo website.

Is my order submitted when I use the form?

Not yet. Cart and checkout are demo-only and stored in your browser. Real order submission will be implemented with the backend.
